# PS01: GlitchVerse – "The World Is Broken"

**Event:** Artificial Arcade (24-hour hackathon), Atharva University, Mumbai
**Category:** Single-Player Puzzle & Adventure
**Team:** 3 members

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Teams must design and develop an innovative single-player game set in a glitched virtual world where players can manipulate the fundamental rules of the environment to solve puzzles and overcome obstacles.

### The game should demonstrate

- Creative environment manipulation (gravity reversal, time freezing, dimension switching, etc.)
- Interactive puzzles and obstacles
- Progressively challenging levels
- Smooth player movement and controls
- Engaging gameplay with a scoring system

## 2. Core Goals (what judges evaluate)

- Creativity and innovation in game mechanics
- Game logic and level design
- Problem-solving and puzzle development
- Player engagement and user experience
- Smooth gameplay and visual presentation

## 3. Compulsory Requirements

- [ ] Minimum 4 playable levels
- [ ] At least 3 unique environment-altering mechanics
- [ ] Interactive puzzles or obstacles in every level
- [ ] Functional level progression and restart system
- [ ] Scoring or timer-based performance tracking
- [ ] Clear win/loss conditions and a user-friendly interface

## 4. Organizer Notes

- Think beyond traditional gaming concepts; introduce unique mechanics, engaging visuals and creative gameplay.
- Focus on building a functional, enjoyable and innovative game **within 24 hours**.

## 5. Our Constraints

- 3 team members, beginner-friendly scope
- 24 hours total
- Rule: **1 meaningful Git commit every 2 hours** (push regularly)
- AI tools are allowed during the build
- Build in 2D first, with a design that could be transformed into 3D later
